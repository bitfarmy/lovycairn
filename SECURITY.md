# Security

LovyCairn runs inside your GNOME Shell session with your user's permissions. It makes no network connections, reads and
writes only your Desktop folder, `~/.config/lovycairn`, `~/.cache/lovycairn` and your notes folder, and starts only `git`,
your terminal, your browser and the notes app.

If you find a vulnerability, please **do not open a public issue**: use GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/bitfarmy/lovycairn/security/advisories/new) for this repository.
